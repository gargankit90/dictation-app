## Short genuine dictation ("yes", about 0.6 s)

Covered at unit level, not in the VM. `issue_172_a_short_press_with_only_key_clicks_is_not_sent` builds 300 ms of noise with a click, a 150 ms tone at −40 dBFS (the "word"), then 300 ms more: 750 ms total, assessed `Transcribe`. 150 ms is 7 frames; the gate needs 5 in a row (100 ms). Limit: a word quieter than the silence threshold or with a sustained part under 100 ms (a very quiet "no") is dropped; no test pins that edge. The VM scenarios use the mock transcriber with a tone fixture, so no scenario dictates a real spoken short word.
