Where your data goes

Connect OpenRouter

OpenRouter sends your audio to OpenAI to turn it into text and routes the text to a provider for cleanup. Vocet doesn’t run a server that receives your dictations.

On this Mac

Your audio and dictation text stay on your Mac.

In both modes your history stays on your Mac. Usage and error reports are on by default; turn them off in Settings → Privacy.

Questions

What does it cost?

Vocet is free during the beta. On this Mac there is nothing else to pay. With Connect OpenRouter you pay OpenRouter for what you use: about $0.70 per 1,000 dictations, with a $5 minimum top-up.

What does Vocet clean up?

Fillers like “um”, false starts and self-corrections, and punctuation. It keeps what you meant and drops how you stumbled getting there.

Why does Vocet need Accessibility access?

To press paste for you in the app you are using, and to notice the hotkey. Vocet does not read what is on your screen.

What if my text doesn’t paste?

Use Copy last. It puts your most recent text back on the clipboard so you can paste it yourself.

Can I see the source code?

Yes, on GitHub. It is source available under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-ALv2). You can read and build the code, and each version becomes Apache 2.0 after two years.
