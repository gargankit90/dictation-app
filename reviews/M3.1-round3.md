**Point 1 – Flags parsed twice**
- **1a)** Line 122 runs in the **first (already‑running) instance** of the application. `setup()` is only called after the single‑instance plugin has prevented a second launch, so `std::env::args()` reflects the arguments of that first launch.
- **1b)** Line 82 is executed inside the `tauri_plugin_single_instance` callback. That callback runs in the **first instance** but receives the `argv` of the **new second launch** that is about to exit. Therefore the `argv` seen at line 82 is **different** from the one used at line 122.
- **1c)** Concrete wrong behaviour: the flag parsing runs twice, producing a duplicate warning about `SECOND_LAUNCH`. The warning appears once in the exiting second process and once in the first instance’s log. This is merely cosmetic and does not affect functionality; it is a **minor** issue.

**Point 2 – Unnecessary #[allow(clippy::cast_precision_loss)]**
The allow is justified. The cast `self.next_u64() >> 11 as f64` always fits within the 53‑bit mantissa of an `f64`; the `reason = "53 bits fit an f64 exactly"` explains this. Removing the allow would trigger a Clippy pedantic warning, causing the quality gate to fail. Therefore the allow is **necessary** and should remain.
