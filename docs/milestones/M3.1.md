# M3.1: retry policy and mock transcriber

## Goal

Retry rules and a configurable mock engine for tests.

## Scope

- `core::retry`: exponential backoff with jitter and max attempts, using an injectable clock.
- `transcribe::mock::MockTranscriber`: fixed text, delay or error; selectable with `--transcriber mock`.

## Out of scope

- Network calls.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Backoff sequence, jitter bounds and max attempts are correct with a fake clock.
2. `[auto]` MockTranscriber returns its text, honours its delay, and returns configured errors.
3. `[auto]` `transcribe` coverage ≥ 85%.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
