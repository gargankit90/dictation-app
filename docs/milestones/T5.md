# T5: test reliability: no flaky, hanging or blind tests

## Goal

Make the test suite trustworthy under load, so a red run always means a real
bug and a green run can't hide one. Fixes the flaky, hanging and blind tests
filed during M5.4–M5.2, plus small doc and CI follow-ups. From a triage of
the open issues on 2026-11-01.

## Scope

- **Flaky under load:** #147 (test-key-handling "INT after the store") and
  #149 (test-runner "the harness is real: e2e_hold_release"). Find the race
  (a timing assumption, a missing wait on a real condition) and fix it; no
  longer sleeps or retries that only hide it.
- **Hangs instead of failing:** #150 (overlay bounded-channel test): give it
  a bound that fails fast with a clear message.
- **Blind checks:** #153 (`textedit_is_empty` passes when TextEdit can't be
  read: an unreadable document must fail the check); #159 (onboarding
  auto-advance not pinned to "granted"); #156 (isolate the vendored modifier
  reconciliation test from the live keyboard state).
- **Coverage gaps worth a cheap test:** #136 (`e2e_layout_paste` should tell
  the paste key apart on AZERTY and Russian too, not just Dvorak), #138
  (`launch_without_focus` unit test outside the VM), #142 (singleton stop
  budget and accept-loop exit), #143 (ACL app settings fixture cleanup).
- **Small docs and CI:** #63, #49 (doc comments), #55, #54 (small core/ports
  refactors), #48 (lint and doctest `dictation-platform` without the
  `testing` feature in CI).

## Out of scope

- Guard tests (#28: owner only). #128 (failure evidence; separate design).
  #31 (proptest distribution) unless trivial.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` #147 and #149 each pass 30 runs in a row while a full gate runs
   alongside (or an equivalent CPU load), and the PR explains the race found.
2. `[auto]` #150's test fails within seconds when coalescing is turned off.
3. `[auto]` #153: with TextEdit unreadable (e.g. not running), the check
   fails; #159 and #156 tests fail on the bug they describe.
4. `[auto]` The new coverage tests (#136, #138, #142) fail when their bug is
   put back.
5. `[auto]` CI runs clippy and doctests for `dictation-platform` without the
   `testing` feature; the gate stays under ~10 minutes.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds,
`Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
