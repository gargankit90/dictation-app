# M5.5a: feels solid — text spacing, layouts, clipboard, launch

## Goal

Fix the small things that make dictation feel buggy: words glued to the
text before the cursor, wrong keys on non-US layouts, an image on the
clipboard lost after dictating, and the Dock icon flashing at launch. Part 1
of M5.5 "Feels solid"; part 2 (M5.5b: #88 hotkey listener restart, #47
stuck hotkey) follows T2.

## Scope

- **#93 smart leading space.** A platform adapter behind a port reads the
  single character before the cursor in the focused app (macOS
  Accessibility: the focused element's `AXValue` and `AXSelectedTextRange`)
  and passes it to `core::text::clean`, so dictating after `word` gives
  `word Hello`, and after `word ` or at the start of a field gives no extra
  space. Privacy: read only what's needed (at most a few characters before
  the cursor), keep it in memory for that one dictation, never log it at any
  level, never send it anywhere. If the app doesn't expose it, or it's a
  secure field, fall back to today's behaviour (no space added). Bound the
  read with a short timeout so a hung app can't delay the paste.
- **#83 non-QWERTY layouts and image clipboard.** Prove the paste shortcut
  uses the right key on AZERTY, Dvorak and Russian input sources (the real
  TIS/UCKeyTranslate lookup), and that an image on the clipboard is restored
  after a dictation. Add VM scenarios: `e2e_layout_paste` (switch the input
  source in the VM, dictate into TextEdit, restore the layout) and
  `e2e_image_clipboard_restore`. Fix anything they find.
- **#59 no Dock flash at launch.** Set the Accessory activation policy before
  the event loop starts (or in `Info.plist` via `LSUIElement`, whichever
  really stops the brief Regular activation), so no Dock icon or menu bar
  flash appears at launch.

## Out of scope

- #88, #47 (M5.5b, after T2). Windows adapters.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Pipeline tests with a fake "text before cursor" port: a space is
   added after a letter or punctuation that needs one, not after a space,
   newline or opening bracket, not at the start of a field, and not when the
   port fails, times out or reports a secure field.
2. `[auto]` A test proves the text read from the focused app never reaches
   any log level (capture all levels with a unique sentinel).
3. `[auto]` `e2e_smart_space`: in the VM, type `hello` in TextEdit, dictate
   `world` → `hello world`; at the start of an empty document → `world`.
4. `[auto]` `e2e_layout_paste` passes for AZERTY, Dvorak and Russian.
5. `[auto]` `e2e_image_clipboard_restore` passes (the image survives).
6. `[manual]` On the owner's Mac: launch the app; no Dock icon or flash.
   Dictate after a word in Slack/Notes: one space is added.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds,
`Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
