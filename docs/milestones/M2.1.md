# M2.1: WAV and audio maths

## Goal

Pure audio helpers in `core`.

## Scope

- `core::wav`: encode 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM to an in-memory WAV.
- `core::audio_math`: RMS, silence detection, duration, minimum-length rule.

## Out of scope

- Capturing audio.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` WAV header fields and byte length are correct; round trip through the `hound` reader.
2. `[auto]` Silence below the RMS threshold is detected; speech above it isn't.
3. `[auto]` Minimum duration rule and empty buffers are handled.
4. `[auto]` `core` coverage stays ≥ 85%.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
