# M5.7: microphone permission without a wrong error

## Goal

A first-time user who presses the hotkey sees the macOS microphone prompt,
and nothing tells them they were denied until they actually are. If they
deny it and later allow it in System Settings, the app tells them plainly
that it needs a relaunch, and offers one. From the open issues on
2026-11-02 (#161), plus a small test-fixture cleanup (#164).

## Scope

- **#161 no "reopen" error while the prompt is up.** While microphone
  permission is undetermined (the macOS prompt is showing), show nothing or
  a neutral "Waiting for microphone permission…"; never paste and never
  leave the app Recording. Show the denial error only after a real denial.
  If the user clicks Allow, the dictation they started may be discarded,
  but the next press must work without a relaunch.
- **#161 relaunch after a later grant.** A grant in System Settings after
  "Don't Allow" doesn't reach the running app (docs/ENGINEERING.md). Make
  the denial message say that the app must be reopened after allowing it,
  and offer a Relaunch action (reuse M5.2's `relaunch_app`) from the tray
  while the microphone is denied. Keep it short enough not to clip in the
  overlay.
- **#164 test fixture cleanup.** Under `KEEP=1`, `tests/e2e/test-runner.sh`
  keeps its files but still terminates fixture processes (success, failure
  and interrupt).

## Out of scope

- Detecting a later grant without a relaunch (macOS doesn't deliver it to
  the running process).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Pipeline/app tests with a fake permission port: undetermined →
   no denial error, Idle, no paste; denied → the denial error; allowed after
   the prompt → the next press records.
2. `[auto]` The Relaunch action appears in the tray only while the
   microphone is denied and calls the existing relaunch path; Vitest or Rust
   test proves it.
3. `[auto]` `e2e_microphone_denied_then_recover` (dictation-vm) still passes
   and checks that no denial error is shown before the prompt is answered.
4. `[auto]` #164: with `KEEP=1`, no fixture process survives the run.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds,
`Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
