# M5.9: hotkeys that don't get in the way

## Goal

Choosing a hotkey should be easy and should never cost the user their
normal shortcuts. From the owner's manual checks on 2026-11-02 (#172,
#170).

## Scope

- **#172 shortcuts on the hotkey's modifier.** With a modifier-only
  hotkey (e.g. `RightCmd`, `RightAlt`), a non-modifier key going down
  while it's held means the user is typing a shortcut: cancel that
  recording (discard; no transcription, no paste, no clipboard change, no
  error shown) and let the shortcut through untouched. Holding the
  modifier alone still dictates. Combination hotkeys (`Ctrl+Shift+Space`)
  keep today's behaviour. If the overlay flashes for a quick shortcut,
  delay showing it briefly (keep it short; recording itself must still
  start at once so no speech is lost). First reproduce the owner's report
  in the VM (right ⌘+C doesn't copy, right ⌘+V doesn't paste) and say in
  the PR what actually broke them.
- **#170 recording the hotkey.** Settings' Hotkey field (and onboarding's,
  if it has one) records the key or combination the user presses and shows
  it readably (e.g. "Right ⌘", "⌃⇧Space"), storing today's text spec
  (`crates/core/src/hotkey_spec.rs`). Sided modifiers are told apart. Esc
  cancels recording; the saved hotkey works at once and after relaunch, as
  today.

## Out of scope

- New hotkey kinds (double-tap, toggle mode).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Matcher/pipeline tests: with `RightCmd` and `RightAlt`, a key
   pressed while the modifier is held cancels the recording with no
   transcription, paste or clipboard change; the modifier alone still
   records; combination hotkeys unchanged.
2. `[auto]` VM scenario: hotkey `RightCmd`; right ⌘+C copies a selection
   and right ⌘+V pastes the user's clipboard into TextEdit, with nothing
   dictated or pasted by the app; holding right ⌘ alone dictates.
3. `[auto]` Vitest: pressing right ⌘ alone in the Hotkey field records
   `RightCmd`; a combination records its spec; Esc cancels; the saved value
   round-trips.
4. `[auto]` VM: a hotkey recorded in Settings works at once and after
   relaunch.
5. `[auto]` Pipeline/core test: a transcript with no letters or digits
   (only punctuation or whitespace, e.g. `.` from a short silent recording)
   is never inserted: it is discarded like silence, with no paste, no
   clipboard change and no error shown (#172, the owner's reproduction).

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above
thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
