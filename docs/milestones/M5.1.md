# M5.1: overlay

## Goal

Show Recording, Transcribing and Error states without stealing focus.

## Scope

- Overlay window as a non-activating panel (`tauri-nspanel`), near the cursor or bottom centre.
- Its capability grants only the events it listens to.

## Out of scope

- Waveform or level meter (later).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Vitest: the overlay renders Recording, Transcribing and Error states.
2. `[auto]` The overlay's capability file grants no commands beyond what it uses.
3. `[manual]` While the overlay shows, the paste still lands in the focused app, not the overlay.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
