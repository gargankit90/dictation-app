# M4.3: full app with debug audio and mock transcriber

## Goal

The whole app working end to end in the VM, with no network.

## Scope

- Wire real hotkey, `FileAudioSource`, mock transcriber and real paste.
- `tests/e2e/run.sh`: restore the golden snapshot, install the build, run the suite, collect logs and screenshots.
- Grant Accessibility and Microphone to the `dictation-app Dev`-signed app in the golden snapshot (one-time; update the snapshot).

## Out of scope

- Real Groq calls.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` `e2e_mock_paste`: "hello world" appears in TextEdit (read back with `osascript`).
2. `[auto]` `e2e_clipboard_restore`: a `pbcopy` sentinel survives a dictation.
3. `[auto]` `e2e_silence`: a silent WAV pastes nothing.
4. `[auto]` `e2e_rapid`: 5 quick press/release cycles give 5 pastes in order and end Idle.
5. `[auto]` `tests/e2e/run.sh` runs the whole suite in the VM with no human input.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
