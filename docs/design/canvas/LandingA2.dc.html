Setup ends with your first real dictation

  1. 1Choose how speech becomes textOn this Mac (a one-time model download) or your own Groq or OpenAI key.
  2. 2Allow two permissionsMicrophone, and Accessibility to paste at your cursor. The model downloads meanwhile.
  3. 3Confirm your keyRight Option by default, or record another.
  4. 4Say a sentenceIt’s recorded, transcribed and pasted — so you know it all works.

Free. No account.

  • Unlimited dictation with a model on your Mac
  • Or your own Groq or OpenAI key — they bill your account directly
  • Open source under the MIT licence
  • No paid plan, no sign-up

Which engine?

English — Parakeet, about 477 MB, fast on Apple Silicon.
Hindi and 29 more languages — Qwen3-ASR, [SIZE].
Your key — Groq or OpenAI Whisper; needs internet.

Where your data goes

Your speechOn this Mac: stays on your Mac. With your key: sent from your Mac straight to Groq or OpenAI. Never through a server of ours.
Your keyStored in your macOS Keychain. Never logged or sent to us.
Usage and error eventsOn by default: anonymous events to PostHog and error reports to Sentry, so we can fix what breaks. Never audio, transcripts, typed text, keys or app names. Turn off in setup or Settings. Full list

Questions

Why does it need Accessibility?

To notice your dictation key in any app and paste at your cursor. It doesn’t read your screen.

How is this different from macOS dictation?

[Answer after measuring: model choice, your own key, one-key hold-to-talk in every app.]

Something isn’t working

Menu bar icon → Troubleshoot checks permissions, your key, the model and the hotkey, and says what to fix. Report a problem shows you the report before you share it.
