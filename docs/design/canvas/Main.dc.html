On this Mac

A speech model runs on your Mac. Download it once ([MODEL SIZE]); after that dictation works offline and audio never leaves your computer.

  • No account, no key
  • Needs disk space and an Apple Silicon Mac

Your own cloud key

Paste a Groq or OpenAI API key. Audio goes straight from your Mac to that provider — never through a server of ours.

  • Key stored in your macOS Keychain
  • The provider may charge for usage on your account

You can switch between the two any time in Settings.

What leaves your Mac

We collect anonymous usage events — like “setup finished” or “a dictation failed with a network error” — so we can see where things break. They are on by default and one switch in Settings turns them off.

Never collected

Audio, transcripts, anything you type, API keys, or the names of apps you dictate into.

Problem reports

Only when you choose Report a problem. You see exactly what’s in it before it goes anywhere.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

Murmur is free and open source. On-Mac transcription is free; a cloud key is billed by the provider on your own account.

Why does it need Accessibility?

To notice the hotkey in any app and paste the text at your cursor. It doesn’t read your screen.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 13 or later.

Something isn’t working

Open the menu bar icon → Troubleshoot. It checks permissions, the hotkey and your model or key, and tells you what to fix.
