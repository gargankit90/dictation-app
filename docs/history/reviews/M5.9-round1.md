Tests reject separated key clicks and retain a 150 ms −40 dBFS word among clicks; existing quiet-signal coverage includes −45 dBFS. These synthetic signals verify the implemented threshold, not every real speaker: a short word without 100 ms continuously above the floor will be dropped. Real microphone quality remains a manual validation limit; I found no concrete real-speech regression to classify as a blocker or major.
