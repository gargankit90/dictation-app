## Developer's notes judged

1. **Missing Accessibility permission logs a `WARN` line and the test flags still work:** Sound for debug and e2e builds (the golden VM has no Accessibility grant until M4.3, and keeping missing Accessibility at startup non-fatal avoids panicking `setup` on first launch before onboarding). In release builds (`not(debug_assertions)`), `--press`/`--release` must be gated off and the warning should not claim test hooks are active (Finding #1).
2. **`GrantedPermissions` stand-in until the real inserter:** Sound. `MacPermissions::check(Permission::Microphone)` currently returns `NotDetermined` until M2.2 lands, and `Runner::missing_permission` checks `Microphone` first on every press, so wiring `MacPermissions` in M1.5 would block every press (real or synthetic) before `Recording`.
3. **`tests/e2e/` written by the Developer at the Lead's request:** Fine; explicitly requested by the Lead and part of `docs/milestones/M1.5.md`'s scope. `dictation-vm-golden` is only ever cloned (`lume clone "$GOLDEN" "$VM"`), never started, modified, or deleted, and no secrets enter logs or scripts (minor remote-shell quoting for future paths with spaces noted in Finding #3 / [#56](https://github.com/gargankit90/dictation-app/issues/56)).
4. **The 800×600 main window opens at launch:** Acceptable as a non-blocking M1.5 carry-over since closing the window keeps the tray app alive, but a menu-bar dictation app should launch quietly in the menu bar without opening a placeholder window or Dock icon (Finding #2 / [#53](https://github.com/gargankit90/dictation-app/issues/53)).
