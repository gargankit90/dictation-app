## Developer's notes

1. **`DEFAULT_SILENCE_THRESHOLD = 0.01` (−40 dBFS) reasoned default:** Sound for M2.1. −40 dBFS sits cleanly between a quiet-room noise floor (−60 to −50 dBFS) and close speech (−35 to −20 dBFS), and `is_silent` treats `NaN` thresholds safely (`false`, never discarding audio). One thing to watch when revisiting with real mic capture in M2.2+: whole-clip RMS dilutes a short utterance over a long silent hold by `sqrt(t_speech / t_clip)` (for example, a 300 ms soft word at −28 dBFS / `0.04` RMS inside a 10 s silent hold averages to ~`0.0069` RMS < `0.01`), so pause-heavy holds are worth checking on real audio.
2. **`MIN_DURATION` reusing `coordinator::MIN_HOLD` (200 ms):** Agreed. Re-exporting a dedicated `audio_math::MIN_DURATION = MIN_HOLD` avoids magic-number drift today while giving callers an audio-specific constant if capture startup latency in M2.2 ever warrants tuning the two independently.
3. **`ports::Audio::duration` duplicating `core::audio_math::duration`:** Leaving `crates/ports` untouched kept this PR cleanly scoped to `crates/core`; tracked as [#55](https://github.com/gargankit90/dictation-app/issues/55) so `ports` delegates to `core`'s exact integer maths in a follow-up.
