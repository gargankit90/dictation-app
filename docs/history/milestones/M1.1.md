# M1.1: coordinator state machine

## Goal

The pure state machine at the heart of the app: `step(state, event) -> (state, Vec<Command>)`.

## Scope

- `core::events`: `Event` (HotkeyPressed, HotkeyReleased, CancelPressed, Tick(now), AudioCaptured, TranscriptReady, TranscriptionFailed, InsertDone, InsertFailed, PermissionMissing) and `Command` (StartRecording, StopRecording, DiscardRecording, Transcribe, Insert, ShowError, StateChanged).
- `core::coordinator`: states Idle → Recording → Transcribing → Inserting → Idle; times come in through events (no clock calls).
- Rules: minimum hold 200 ms (shorter taps ignored); maximum recording 5 minutes; Esc cancels; a press while Transcribing or Inserting is ignored; errors return to Idle with a user-facing error.
- `core::error` with `thiserror`.

## Out of scope

- Any I/O, async or OS code. Toggle mode (later).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Press then release (≥ 200 ms) emits StartRecording, then StopRecording and Transcribe.
2. `[auto]` A tap shorter than 200 ms emits DiscardRecording and returns to Idle.
3. `[auto]` A press while Transcribing or Inserting is ignored (no commands, state unchanged).
4. `[auto]` CancelPressed during Recording discards the audio and returns to Idle.
5. `[auto]` A Tick at 5 minutes stops the recording and transcribes it.
6. `[auto]` TranscriptionFailed, an empty transcript or InsertFailed returns to Idle with ShowError.
7. `[auto]` PermissionMissing on press emits ShowError and never starts recording.
8. `[auto]` `proptest`: for random event sequences the machine never panics, always reaches Idle after the in-flight work completes, and never leaves a recording running.
9. `[auto]` `core` coverage ≥ 85%; `core` has no workspace dependencies.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
