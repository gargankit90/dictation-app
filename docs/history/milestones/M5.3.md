# M5.3: logging and launch at login

## Goal

Logs testers can send us, without leaking anything.

## Scope

- `tracing` with a rotating log file in the app's log dir; "Reveal log" in the tray.
- Redaction: keys and transcripts never logged at info level or above.
- Launch-at-login toggle in settings.

## Out of scope

- Remote log upload.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[auto]` Unit tests prove keys and transcript text are redacted from log output.
2. `[auto]` Log rotation keeps size bounded.
3. `[manual]` Launch at login works after a reboot of the VM.

Every milestone also needs: the gate green, coverage at or above thresholds, `Tester: PASS` and `Reviewer: APPROVED` on the PR.
