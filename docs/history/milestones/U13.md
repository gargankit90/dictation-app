# U13: Recording pill fixes

Build spec from the Design Lead, 2026-11-05. A small PR, separate from setup: it changes the real pill everywhere, and U11's Welcome demo reuses the result. Merge it before U11 if you can; U11 doesn't depend on it, because the demo reuses the component.

The owner cares most about the pill. These are the changes I'd make, and what I'd leave alone.

## Why

Reviewing the code (`src/overlay/view.ts`, `crates/core/src/level.rs`, `crates/app/src/overlay.rs`) found two real problems the screenshots hid. Both come from my U4 spec.

1. **A thinking pause says "No sound · Check your microphone".** `SilenceWatch` counts any quiet run of 1.5 s, including after you've been talking. People pause for that long mid-sentence, so a working microphone gets told to check itself.
2. **The bars barely move with speech.** Height is linear from −60 to −10 dBFS, so normal speech (about −40 to −20) uses only about 6 of the 14 px, and a quiet room (−60 to −50) never looks flat. It reads as a block.

## Changes

| # | What | Today | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | When "No sound" shows | Any 1.5 s quiet run | **Only if nothing has been heard yet in this hold, after 2.5 s.** Once speech has been heard, a pause stays on Listening with flat bars. Back to Listening at the first sound, as today. |
| 2 | Meter range | −60 to −10 dBFS | **−50 to −20 dBFS.** Silence at or below the "No sound" threshold is flat (−50 is `NO_SOUND_THRESHOLD_DBFS`), and speech uses the full height. Keep the 7 bars, 4-18 px, scrolling, and the 50 ms transition. |
| 3 | Success label | "Paste sent" | **"Done"**, same check and 800 ms. You don't "send" a paste; to a person it reads like a system message. "Done" says Vocet finished its part and doesn't claim the text landed, which is the reason "Pasted" was ruled out. |
| 4 | Error time | 4 s | **6 s.** Our sentences are about 15 words and name a fix, so 4 s isn't enough to read one. The same problem stays in the menu until it's fixed. |

## Decided: leave alone

- **"Listening" and "Release to paste · Esc to cancel".** Clear, and the only place people learn Esc. Fading the hint after a few dictations is a good idea for after 0.1.0 (it needs a stored count): please file it as an issue.
- **The static red dot.** No pulse. The bars are the live signal, and a pulse would move even when the microphone hears nothing, which is exactly the false reassurance the meter replaced.
- **"Transcribing…" with the spinner**, and "No sound · Check your microphone (device)".
- **"Pasted" in setup's Try it**, where the practice box verifies it. It stays.
- **The internal state name and log line `paste_sent`,** so analytics and e2e log matches don't change. Only the visible label changes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. [auto] `SilenceWatch` reports silent only when no level has been loud since the hold began and quiet has lasted 2.5 s. After any loud level it never reports silent again in that hold. Tests with a fake clock: silence from the start (No sound at 2.5 s, not before); speech then a 5 s pause (never No sound); silence then speech (back to Listening). `e2e_pill_states` is updated.
2. [auto] `METER_LOW_DBFS` = −50 and `METER_HIGH_DBFS` = −20. `barHeight` tests: −50 and below → 4 px; −20 and above → 18 px; −35 → 11 px.
3. [auto] The success state's visible label is "Done", with the check, for 800 ms (±100). No "Paste sent" remains in user-facing strings, the app's tests or docs (`docs/privacy.md` if mentioned). `paste_sent` stays as the internal name. Screen readers hear "Done".
4. [auto] `ERROR_VISIBLE_FOR` is 6 s. The sentence still stays in the menu until the problem is resolved.
5. [auto] `site/index.html`: the demo badges that read "Pasted" read "Done", so the site matches the app. Do this on top of #275 once it's merged, or leave it to the Design Lead's next site PR and say so.
6. [manual] A short screen recording on the PR, on a real or VM microphone: speak, pause for 3 s mid-sentence (stays on Listening), keep speaking, release. And one where you stay silent (No sound at about 2.5 s). The Design Lead checks how the bars look.

## Out of scope

Hint fading (issue, after 0.1.0), a pill redesign, other languages for these strings.
