# U11: Setup redesign from the owner's run on a real Mac

Build spec from the Design Lead, 2026-11-05. **Depends on:** U9, U10 and U12's own-key form (`src/settings/ownKey.tsx`), merged first. Don't redo those.

**Mock (light and dark, Welcome animated):** ~/Documents/dictation-design/design-system/u11-mock.html, built from tokens.css. **Owner's screenshots:** /Users/gauri/Desktop, 2026-11-05, 6.56-7.36 AM.

**Don't change:** Permissions (the owner said it looks good), Ready (U10 owns its switch; only its engine line changes, see criterion 8), the step order, the rail.

## 1. Welcome: show what Vocet does

The owner: "welcome user to the product by showing them what it does". The screen asks for nothing and says nothing about data (owner decision, U10).

- Title: **"Talk instead of type"**. Line below it (15 px, `--vc-ink-2`): **"Vocet turns what you say into text, in any app."**
- A small demo card that plays on a loop, about 8 s. **It reuses the real pill component (`OverlayContent` in `src/overlay/Overlay.tsx`) and its real CSS, fed by a script,** so the wording and look can't drift from the app:
  1. **Hold:** a keycap reading **"Right Option (⌥)"**, caption "Hold the key". The real pill appears: dot, meter, "Listening", "Release to paste · Esc to cancel".
  2. **Speak:** caption "Say what you mean". The bars follow a fixed, speech-shaped list of levels: syllables with short gaps, never random numbers.
  3. **Let go:** caption "Let go. The words land at your cursor". The pill shows "Transcribing…" (about 1 s), then the words land in the box as it shows the check and its real success label (about 0.8 s), then it goes. Same sequence as the real pill, so we don't promise instant text.
- The current beat's caption is emphasised. It's an illustration only: no microphone and no real level data.
- The pill is shown at its real size, including the hint, at 740×560 and at the 680×520 minimum. Widen the demo card if it needs room; it must not wrap or scale the pill.
- With Reduce Motion: no loop; show the final frame with all three captions.
- The keycap and its text align on one baseline (the owner's complaint about today's row). If the user already chose another key (Run setup again), show that key in the long form, e.g. "Right Command (⌘)".
- One primary action: **"Get started"**. No secondary actions.

The demo card is `aria-hidden`; give the screen one sentence for screen readers: "Hold Right Option, say what you mean, and let go. The words land at your cursor."

## 2. Speech: choosing own key no longer opens a form

- Same language chips and two choices, without the form underneath. Selecting "Use my own Groq or OpenAI key" only selects it; the primary reads **"Continue"**, and it opens the next screen.
- The on-device choice keeps "Download and continue" (or "Continue" if downloaded).
- **Plain model names, the same as Settings,** from one shared function with real sizes and counts. The technical model name doesn't appear in setup. Owner-approved wording (2026-11-05):
  - English chosen: **"English · 477 MB"**, "Runs on this Mac. Works offline after a one-time download."
  - Hindi and others: **"30 languages · 590 MB"**, "Runs on this Mac. Works offline after a one-time download."
  - Key card: **"Use my own Groq or OpenAI key"**, "About 100 languages. Needs internet. Your speech is sent to the provider you choose."
  - A language no on-device model covers: **"Gujarati needs your own key. Vocet's on-device models don't cover it yet."** (with the real language name), and the key card is the selected choice.
  - Don't write "All languages" or "100+", and make no promise about accuracy per language.

## 3. Own key: its own screen

The rail stays on **Speech**. Back returns to the two choices, keeping them as they were.

- Title **"Connect your key"**; line below: "Vocet sends your speech to this provider, using your own account with them."
- Reuse U12's form: a Provider row with a compact popup, the 3-step how-to, the key field, **Test & Save**, and one status line.
- **No key saved yet:** Continue is disabled, with the reason "Continue unlocks after Test & Save succeeds".
- **A key is saved:** no key field at first. Show **"✓ Key saved"**, "In your macOS Keychain. It's never shown again.", and **Replace**, which reveals the field and Test & Save. Continue works; as today, it tests the saved key first and shows the failure on this screen.
- **No Remove key in setup.** It stays in Settings.
- Changing Provider re-reads whether that provider has a saved key.
- All existing key protections in `ownKey.tsx` stay. U12 keeps the sheet presentation for Settings; here the same form renders in a page.

## 4. Try it

- **No Reset to default.** The row is "Your dictation key", the keycap, and a small plain **"Change key"** button, on one line, all at the 680×520 minimum.
- Change key starts the recorder: "Press a key…" with **Cancel**. Use the recorder option U12 adds, without Use default here. Focus returns to the box afterwards (U9).
- Instruction: **"Hold the key, say a sentence, let go."** It replaces "Click the box, hold the key…", because focus now returns by itself (U9).
- Stages, failed-paste panel, Skip for now and the disabled reason stay as they are.

## 5. "Right Option (⌥)"

The long key form appears on Welcome at least. Elsewhere the keycap's short form ("Right ⌥") stays. Add one `keyLongLabel(spec)` next to `keycapLabel`, with tests for left and right modifiers and combinations. No user-facing text shows raw specs like `RightAlt`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. [auto] Welcome has no switch, no notice and no input, only the title, line, demo and "Get started". A test fails if sharing text appears on it.
2. [auto] The demo renders the real pill component in each state, with the hint, driven by a fixed level list (a test checks the labels and that no `Math.random` is used). With `prefers-reduced-motion` it shows the static final frame with no timers running. It uses no microphone, no level events and no real dictation. It stops when the step is left (no timers leak). The pill doesn't wrap at 680×520.
3. [auto] Welcome's keycap shows `keyLongLabel` of the stored key. Keycap and text share a baseline (e2e_onboarding_layout or a layout check).
4. [auto] Selecting own key on Speech shows no form; Continue opens "Connect your key". Back returns with the choice and language kept; the rail shows Speech on both screens.
5. [auto] Model names and sizes in setup and Settings → Speech come from one shared function, and a test asserts they match. No catalogue name appears in setup. Tests cover the English, 30-language and uncovered-language cards with the exact wording above; the count comes from `language_codes`.
6. [auto] Own-key screen, no key: Continue is disabled with its reason until Test & Save succeeds. Key saved: "✓ Key saved" and Replace show, with no field until Replace; there's no Remove key anywhere in setup. Continue tests a saved key, and a failure stays on the screen.
7. [auto] Try it has no Reset to default. Change key records and cancels, and focus returns to the box either way. The instruction text matches. A two-key shortcut still fits on one line at 680×520.
8. [auto] Permissions and Ready render unchanged (existing tests pass with no edits to their expectations, beyond step wiring). One exception, the Design Lead's call in review: Ready's engine line uses the same plain names as Speech, `On this Mac · English · ✓ Ready` and `On this Mac · 30 languages · ✓ Ready` (#299).
9. [auto] e2e_onboarding_first_run, e2e_onboarding_five_steps and e2e_onboarding_screenshots are updated for the new screens.
10. [manual] Window-only screenshots in light and dark on the PR: Welcome (one frame per beat, plus Reduce Motion), Speech, both own-key states, and Try it at the default size and 680×520. The Design Lead compares them with the mock and posts OK or CHANGES.

## Out of scope

The sharing switch (U10), setup blockers and language picker styling (U9), Settings (U12), saved audio (#278).
