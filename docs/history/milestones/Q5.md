# Q5: Privacy policy for 0.2.0

Owner decisions, 2026-11-20:
- **Phased plan for training data:** 0.2.0 sends **no** recordings or text for training. No upload service,
  no "share to improve" switch in this release. (Later phases, after a lawyer review: licensed data and
  volunteers first, then an opt-in contribution flow.)
- **Setup analytics:** keep today's behaviour (setup events are sent before the sharing switch appears) and
  **disclose it plainly** in the policy and the app's Privacy text.
- Research to use: `/Users/gauri/vocet-screens/u9/privacy-research.md` (competitors, recommended outline and
  draft wording), `privacy-codex-opinion.md`, `privacy-codex-opinion-2.md`.

## Scope
1. A plain-language policy at `site/privacy.html` (new standalone page, same styles as the site; WEB1 owns
   `site/index.html`: only add a footer link there after WEB1 merges, or coordinate) and `docs/privacy.md`
   kept in sync (one source; the other links to it or is generated).
2. Contents, plain words first:
   - Who: the developer of Vocet (Ankit Garg, Vocet Labs), a privacy contact email (ask the Lead which address).
   - Five-line summary at the top.
   - **What leaves your Mac, per mode** (table): Connect OpenRouter — audio goes through OpenRouter to OpenAI
     for transcription; the transcript and your custom words (if any) go through OpenRouter to a cleanup
     provider; Vocet sends `data_collection: deny` on every request and `zdr: true` on cleanup requests;
     Vocet has no server that receives dictations; the user's own OpenRouter account and its terms apply.
     On this Mac only — audio and text stay on the Mac (the one-time model downloads come from Hugging Face).
   - **Retention by providers:** state only what is verified from the providers' current published docs for
     the exact route (OpenRouter's own logging/retention, OpenAI's transcription endpoint, the cleanup host);
     link the sources; if a period can't be verified, say "governed by OpenRouter's and the provider's
     policies" rather than inventing a number.
   - **No training:** Vocet does not use your recordings or text to train models, and asks providers not to
     (data_collection deny).
   - **Usage and error reports:** what PostHog receives (the closed event list) and what Sentry receives on a
     crash (scrubbed location/stack, no audio, text, keys, app names or paths); on by default; how to turn
     off; **setup events are sent during setup before the switch is shown** (disclosed).
   - Local data: History (last 25 by default, on the Mac), debug-mode recordings (only when debug mode is on;
     where they are; how to delete), the Keychain key, logs (never transcripts or keys).
   - Updates check (what is sent: version, nothing personal), Hugging Face downloads.
   - Your choices and rights: turn reports off, delete history, Disconnect OpenRouter, uninstall, contact us
     to ask about data; no account with Vocet exists. Age: not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EU).
   - Changes to the policy (dated; material changes announced in release notes).
3. Fix every current promise that 0.2.0 makes false (site, app Privacy screen, docs): "transcripts never
   sent", "never through a server of ours" (keep: true for 0.2.0), "app names never sent", "open source".
4. App: Settings → Privacy shows a one-line summary of the current mode's data flow and links to the policy.

Out of scope: any training-data feature, Vocet Cloud, a lawyer-reviewed DPA (note in the PR that a lawyer
should review before training data or Vocet Cloud ships).
